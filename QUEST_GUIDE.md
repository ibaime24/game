# Quest Guide

Every quest in the game: how to start it, where it leads, and every stage of it, as the journal records it.

- **Quest gold:** finishing any quest pays **100 gold**, on top of its own rewards.
- **Stage numbers** are the game's internal stage ids (they appear in saves and in `QUESTS.md`). The journal only ever moves forward. Where a quest has several ending stages, you only reach one of them.
- ✔ marks a stage that completes the quest.
- For design notes (characters, places, choices and flags), see [QUESTS.md](QUESTS.md).

## The Main Quest

The Emperor is dead and Oblivion is opening. Six quests, about an hour and a half.

<a id="mq_amulet"></a>
### Deliver the Amulet (`mq_amulet`)

- **Type:** Main, act I · about 10 min
- **How to start:** Starts automatically when you begin a new game and step out of the Imperial Sewers.
- **Leads to:** [Breaking the Siege of Kvatch](#mq_gate)

| Stage | Journal |
|---|---|
| 10 | Emperor Uriel Septim VII lies dead in the sewers beneath the Imperial City, struck down by Mythic Dawn assassins. With his dying breath he pressed the Amulet of Kings into my hands and bade me take it to a man named Jauffre, at Weynon Priory near Chorrol. |
| 100 ✔ | I gave the Amulet of Kings to Jauffre, Grandmaster of the Blades. |

<a id="mq_gate"></a>
### Breaking the Siege of Kvatch (`mq_gate`)

- **Type:** Main, act I · about 20–30 min
- **How to start:** Deliver the Amulet of Kings to Jauffre at Weynon Priory (south-east of Chorrol); he sends you to Kvatch.
- **Leads to:** [Find the Heir](#mq_heir)

| Stage | Journal |
|---|---|
| 10 | Jauffre told me that an Oblivion Gate has torn open before the city of Kvatch. Daedra pour from it unchecked. I should find Captain Savlian Matius at the siege camp below the city. |
| 20 | Savlian Matius says the gate cannot be closed from this side. Someone must enter the Deadlands and take the Sigil Stone that anchors it. I must enter the Oblivion Gate. |
| 30 | I have entered the Deadlands. Far ahead a tower of black stone vents fire into the sky — the Sigil Keep. The Sigil Stone must be at its heart. |
| 40 | I tore the Sigil Stone from its fiery column and the Gate collapsed around me. I am back in Tamriel. I should tell Savlian Matius. |
| 50 | Savlian Matius and his soldiers can hold Kvatch now. I should return to Jauffre at Weynon Priory with the news. |
| 100 ✔ | Jauffre named me a Hero of Kvatch and a friend of the Blades. The jaws of Oblivion have been shut — for now. And then he told me who the Emperor's heir is. |

## Side stories

Standalone stories around the Heartland.

### The Unfinished Map (`sq_map`)

- **Type:** Side story · about 30–60 min
- **How to start:** Talk to Ysolde Vane at the Grey Mare inn in Chorrol.

| Stage | Journal |
|---|---|
| 10 | Ysolde Vane, an old cartographer coughing her life away at the Grey Mare in Chorrol, gave me the last map drawn by her partner Corvin Ashby, who vanished forty years ago. Three marks in his hand: a ring of stones in the hills west of Chorrol, a bell drawn in the waters of Lake Rumare, and a tree with an eye. She wants to know what became of him. I'll start with the stones. |
| 20 | On the altar of the Hollow Stones I found a waymark Corvin left for Ysolde: the door "hears only the bell's true note, and only in the hour that belongs to no day." He left a bellwright's fork on the altar for her — but it is gone. The second mark is a bell in the south-western shallows of Lake Rumare. |
| 30 | In the drowned chapel I found the bellwright's tuning fork — and a torn journal page in a small, neat hand. I have seen that hand before, in the margins of Corvin's map. The last mark is a tree with an eye, in the woods south-west of the Sewers exit. Someone lives there. |
| 40 | Old Marrow says the hour that belongs to no day is midnight: the door in the Hollow Stones answers only between eleven and one. The Ayleids counted the night from moonrise — Moon, Stars, Sun. And Corvin did not go to the stones alone. A woman went with him, and came back down the path by herself. |
| 50 | At midnight I struck the tuning fork against the altar of the Hollow Stones, and a door of light opened in the hill. Nenalatha — the city that keeps time. Corvin went in forty years ago. |
| 60 | The way into Nenalatha's Hall of Hours is barred by a gate of fused light. Three pedestals stand before it: Sun, Moon and Star. Corvin's waymark and Old Marrow agreed on the order the elves counted their night. |
| 70 | The seal is broken. Beyond lies the Hall of Hours — and something still keeps watch there. |
| 75 | The Archivist is destroyed. In the heart of the hall stands a welkynd crystal, and inside it, not a day older than in Ysolde's sketches, is Corvin Ashby. Beside him, on a lectern, the Atlas of Hours feeds the crystal its light. If I strike the crystal with the fork, the Atlas will burn out and Corvin will walk free. If I take the Atlas, the crystal will go dark — and him with it. |
| 80 | I shattered the crystal. Corvin Ashby lives — forty years late, and determined to see Ysolde before the end. I should bring him to the Grey Mare in Chorrol. |
| 85 | I took the Atlas of Hours. The crystal went dark, and Corvin with it. I should go back to Ysolde at the Grey Mare and tell her... something. |
| 100 ✔ | Corvin and Ysolde met again at the Grey Mare. She confessed that she had sealed him in Nenalatha, and he forgave her. She died a little before dawn, holding his hand. Corvin gave me his ring, and the map — finished at last, in two hands. |
| 110 ✔ | I told Ysolde what I found in Nenalatha. She confessed that she had sealed Corvin in the ruin herself, forty years ago, and died that night with the secret finally spoken. The Atlas of Hours is mine. |

| 20 | Bram says he left the Priory road to cut through the woods south of Chorrol, along an old charcoal-burners' track, and came to a clearing. | 30 | I found the clearing: a burned-out cottage, two cold kilns and two stone cairns, all of it eighty years overgrown. Bram's crate is here somewhere. If the lantern woman walks at night, I should come back after dark — eight in the evening until five in the morning. |
| 40 | The lantern woman is a ghost, and she does not know it. She thinks the woods are still burning and that her son Tam, seven years old, is lost somewhere in the smoke. She begged me to find him. He has a wooden fox his father carved. I should bring Bram's crate back to Seed-Neeus and tell her what walks in her woods. |
| 100 ✔ | I told Seed-Neeus about the ghost in the clearing and returned her delivery crate. She paid me — and asked me not to stop. "A ghost that wants something keeps wanting it," she said. "Find out what she needs." |

## The White Hare — the Wintersmeet questline

An endless winter in the north, and a festival at the end of it. Five quests, about an hour and a half.

<a id="sq_hare1"></a>
### The Hare That Didn't Come (`sq_hare1`)

- **Type:** Wintersmeet questline, part 1 · about 10–15 min
- **How to start:** Take the north road out of Chorrol into the snow to Wintersmeet, and talk to Sigrun Warmhands at the Snow Hare inn.
- **Leads to:** [Tracks in the Snow](#sq_hare2)

| Stage | Journal |
|---|---|
| 10 | Every winter, on the morning of the first snowfall, a white hare appears on the roof of the Snow Hare inn in Wintersmeet, and stays until the thaw. This year the first snow came early and thick — and the hare never came. The snow has not stopped since. Sigrun Warmhands is worried, and so, she says, is Old Brynja, who knows more about the hare than anyone. |
| 20 | Old Brynja says the white hare is no ordinary hare but a blessing of Kynareth, Lady of the Sky, who brings the first snow down the mountain and sleeps out the summer at the Skyward Shrine, high on the slope north-west of the village. If something has happened to her, the shrine is the place to look. Brynja gave me the old Lightwatch hymn to read on the way. |
| 30 | The Skyward Shrine is buried in drifts. Someone has been here: there is a sprung snare by the altar, set with the craft of a real trapper. I should search the shrine for anything else. |
| 40 | By the snare I found a tuft of white fur that glows faintly in the dark, and a trapper's tag carved with a name: VARN. The hare was taken alive. I should bring what I found to Brynja. |
